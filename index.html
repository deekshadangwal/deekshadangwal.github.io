About me

I am a security researcher working on AI safety, with a background in computer architecture and hardware security. My current work is on compute verification and on training-time safety monitoring. See my Research page for details; if you are interested in either project, please reach out. My work in computer architecture, privacy, and secure computation has appeared in ISCA, ASPLOS, PLDI, FPL, BMVC, and TMLR.

I am a Fellow at MATS Research in Berkeley, CA, where I work on compute verification with James Petrie (Future of Life Institute). Through the ERA:AI Fellowship in Technical AI Governance (Cambridge, UK), I work on training-time safety monitoring with Alan Cooney (Google DeepMind). Before these fellowships, I co-developed neural tracing for mechanistic interpretability of LLMs and bio-foundation models with collaborators at the Allen Institute, and I was a research scientist at Meta Reality Labs Research, where I worked on energy-efficient system design for smart glasses, system-level power modeling for wearable devices, and privacy research for extended reality.

I received my PhD from UC Santa Barbara, where I worked with Prof. Tim Sherwood on the design of private computer systems. My dissertation, “A System-level Framework for Privacy”, introduced trace wringing, a technique for sharing program behavior with verifiable bounds on information leakage. I am a Rising Stars in EECS alumna, my SoK on hardware-software-security codesign was cited in the US National Strategy on Microelectronics Research, and trace wringing was recognized as an IEEE Micro Top Pick.

I am on the job market for research positions in industry and academia, including faculty positions. Please feel free to reach out at deekshadangwal@gmail.com.
